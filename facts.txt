The client must pay an annual advisory fee equal to 1.00% of assets under management, billed quarterly in arrears.
The advisor may exercise discretionary authority to buy and sell securities in the client's account without obtaining prior approval for each transaction.
The client may terminate the advisory agreement at any time by providing 30 days' written notice.
The advisor must act as a fiduciary and place the client's interests ahead of its own when providing investment advice.
The client is responsible for promptly notifying the advisor of changes to financial circumstances, investment objectives, or risk tolerance.
The advisor does not guarantee investment performance or protection against loss, and the client assumes the risks associated with investing.
Client assets must be held by a qualified independent custodian rather than directly by the advisor.
The advisor must disclose material conflicts of interest, including compensation received from third parties for recommending particular products or services.
Both parties must keep nonpublic personal and financial information confidential except when disclosure is authorized or required by law.
Disputes arising under the agreement must be resolved through binding arbitration in the jurisdiction specified by the agreement.
